What the AI SWITCH Stage is for
The AI SWITCH Stage turns a business problem and its application context into a Palantir-ready implementation contract. It tells Palantir and Rackspace what to build, how it connects, and what is still missing—without claiming that anything has already been deployed.
Step What it does You give You get
1 · Pick a path Matches your situation to one of three starting conditions Your situation The right questions, not all of them
2 · Import Fetches the project. Never runs it. A FLEX app, an OpenCenter container, a repo, a bundle — or nothing An inventory of what you actually have
3 · Describe the problem Turns pain points into AI tools worth building What hurts, what goes in, what should come out, who else it touches Tools that each cite the pain that justified them
4 · Score the gaps Checks 19–24 controls across five areas Owners, data classification, model licence A score, a confidence figure, and named gaps
5 · Plan Picks the service, the cluster and the architecture Nothing more — it is derived Target architecture, diagram, Palantir mapping, passport
6 · Hand off Packages everything for the delivery team — One checksummed ZIP: report, plan, mapping, passport
What it does do
Plans, scores and evidences. Produces a handoff a delivery team can act on.
What it does not
It does not deploy, run your code, or watch live traffic. Everything is marked PROPOSED.
Why the score is honest
Anything unverified reads NOT_CHECKED and lowers confidence — it never counts as a pass.
