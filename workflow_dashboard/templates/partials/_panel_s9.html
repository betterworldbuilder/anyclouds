Enable GitOps on your repo

API: GET /api/gitops/register-snippets?customer=&stamp=&git_url=&branch=&flux_namespace=. Default git_url from GITOPS_PUBLIC_REPO_URL. Repo templates: gitops/templates/ in this project.
