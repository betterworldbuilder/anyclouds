When Should Customers Move to FLEX?

Migration Triggers

Customers should consider moving when one or more OSPC, VMware, hyperscaler, sovereignty, or modernization triggers appear:

1
Current platform limits performance or scalability

OSPC ages, VMware clusters constrain capacity, or hyperscaler architecture becomes too complex.

2
Infrastructure cost needs optimization

VMware renewal costs rise, hyperscaler bills become unpredictable, or utilization needs improvement.

3
New projects are being launched

AI pilots, Kubernetes, analytics, sovereign cloud, or modernization programs should start on FLEX directly.

4
Reduce migration risk with phased move

FLEX supports discover → assess → migrate → validate → cut over from OSPC, VMware, or hyperscaler estates. No big-bang required.

5
Need a future-ready cloud foundation

Hybrid cloud, open-source flexibility, data sovereignty, or long-term workload portability — FLEX is the right platform to evaluate now.

Migration Trigger Conditions

Technical and business conditions driving migration from OSPC, VMware, hyperscaler, or legacy environments to FLEX.

Condition / trigger Why it matters Example use case
Need for modern, OpenStack-based infrastructure FLEX runs on current-generation OpenStack with newer APIs, better automation, and open cloud standards, giving customers an alternative to aging OSPC, VMware dependency, or hyperscaler-only operating models. A company operating legacy apps on OSPC and VMware wants Terraform and Kubernetes-friendly automation without moving every workload into a hyperscaler account.

A DevOps team wants to move hardened Linux and Windows images out of VMware or a hyperscaler into FLEX while preserving security baselines and operational consistency.
Scaling beyond legacy limits or regions FLEX provides scalable capacity and modern hardware when OSPC is constrained, VMware clusters are expensive to expand, or hyperscaler growth is creating runaway spend. A SaaS provider needs regional growth but wants predictable economics, less licensing exposure, and fewer hyperscaler egress surprises.
Push toward cost-efficient, license-free infrastructure FLEX helps reduce proprietary virtualization exposure, hyperscaler bill volatility, and lock-in by using an open-source-based private cloud model. A financial services firm wants to reduce VMware renewal pressure and repatriate steady-state cloud workloads from a hyperscaler into managed open infrastructure.
Requirement for advanced automation and CI/CD pipelines FLEX integrates with Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, and GitOps workflows while avoiding brittle legacy tooling and hyperscaler-specific automation lock-in. A platform team wants consistent pipelines across VMware exits, OSPC migrations, and selected hyperscaler repatriation workloads.
Compliance, security, or data sovereignty upgrade FLEX supports stronger segmentation, isolation, and data placement control for customers facing sovereignty, residency, regulated workload, or hyperscaler data-control concerns. A healthcare or public-sector organization needs data residency, isolation, and compliance alignment that is harder to prove across sprawling hyperscaler accounts.
Long-term roadmap and platform exit strategy FLEX gives customers a managed modernization path for OSPC migration, VMware exit planning, hyperscaler repatriation, or hybrid cloud rebalancing. A customer phases legacy WebLogic, VMware-hosted apps, and steady-state hyperscaler workloads into FLEX over several migration waves.
